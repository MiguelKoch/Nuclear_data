Nuclear reactor construction starts, 1951–2026

Reactors starting construction each year worldwide. Starts peaked in 1976, before Three Mile Island, and fell to near zero by the mid-1990s.

Construction starts by year, 1951 to 2026, with Three Mile Island (1979) and Chernobyl (1986) marked.

Counts are by each reactor's original construction-start year across all 835 units in the dataset, including units later abandoned or closed. Construction restarts are not counted again. 2026 covers starts up to 16 October 2026.

The 3-year average is centred (year before, year, year after); the first and last years average the two years available.

Source: World Nuclear Industry Status Report — interactive dataviz (data as of 16 October 2026).
