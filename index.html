Nuclear reactor construction starts, 1951–2026

Reactors starting construction each year worldwide, by what became of them. Starts peaked in 1976, before Three Mile Island, and fell to near zero by the mid-1990s. About one in six starts from the 1970s and one in four from the 1980s was never finished.

Construction starts by year, 1951 to 2026, split by outcome, with Three Mile Island (1979) and Chernobyl (1986) marked.

Counts are by each reactor's original construction-start year across all 835 units in the dataset. Completed = connected to the grid at some point (including units since closed); abandoned = construction stopped before grid connection (95 units); still under construction = 71 units as of 16 October 2026. Construction restarts are not counted again.

The 3-year average is centred (year before, year, year after), applied to each category separately; the first and last years average the two years available.

Source: World Nuclear Industry Status Report — interactive dataviz (data as of 16 October 2026).
